FAQ

Before you sign up

Short answers to the usual questions.

When does it start?

Cohort A starts on Saturday, November 7 and runs for three consecutive days. Registration opened on Wednesday, October 28 and closes on November 2.

Is the bootcamp really free?

Yes. There is no registration fee and no hidden cost.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Day 1 is conceptual and the AI tools do most of the heavy lifting. Curiosity and a laptop are enough.

What do I need to join?

A laptop or computer, a stable internet connection, a Gmail account for Google Meet and a Telegram account for our community group.

How do I join each session?

We email you the Google Meet link, and post it on Telegram, one hour before each live session.

Will I get the recordings?

Yes. Every registered participant receives the session recording and homework by email after each live day.

What if my cohort is full?

Seats are limited to 250 per cohort. Once a cohort is full you are placed in the next one, and we confirm your dates by email.
